Install Dragon Cookbook on iOS

Dragon Cookbook requires iOS 27 or later.

  1. Open Dragon Cookbook Tap the button below to open Dragon Cookbook in a new tab.
  2. Open the Share menu In Safari, tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Add to Home Screen Scroll down, choose “Add to Home Screen”, then tap “Add”.
Open Dragon Cookbook

Note: The app may say “made in Bolt.” You can just click the X on that message and hide it.

Install Dragon Cookbook on Android

Dragon Cookbook requires Android 15, 16 or 17.

  1. Open Dragon Cookbook Tap the button below to open Dragon Cookbook in a new tab.
  2. Open the browser menu In Chrome, tap the three-dot menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Install the app Choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”, then follow the prompt.
Open Dragon Cookbook

Note: The app may say “made in Bolt.” You can just click the X on that message and hide it.

Dragon Cookbook is mobile-only

Dragon Cookbook is designed to work on mobile devices. You can still visit the website from this computer if you'd like.
